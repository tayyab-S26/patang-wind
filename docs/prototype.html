UK beach game planner · updated Fri 9 Oct, 04:03

arrow = wind direction · circle = verdict (green GO / amber ☔ wet or split / grey no) · tap a beach for its hourly

Live animated wind from Windy — drag the time bar at the bottom. This is Windy's own map (no beach highlighting); use the Map tab for your spots.

Each day is judged on the two models you check — XCWeather (GFS) and the Met Office (UKMO), via Open-Meteo. GREEN only when both agree it's a full offshore day (9+ hrs of 8am–8pm); amber when they split. An agreed day still drops to ☔ wet when either forecasts 0.5mm+ of rain for 3+ of the flying hours — the wind call stands, it just isn't worth the drive. Past about day 8 rain is GFS only (the Met Office model stops there). ECMWF rides along silently so we can later check whose call comes true at each beach. Gusts are the least certain part — read it as a ranking, not a promise. Auto-updates planned 4×/day.
