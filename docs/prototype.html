Windy's own map and forecast. Pick a beach above: the strip at the bottom shows its dates and every hour's wind, gusts and direction (arrows). Drag the time bar to move the map through the days.

Each day is judged on the two apps you check, using the numbers they show: the XCWeather column is NOAA's GFS model on the same 0.25° grid XCWeather draws from, and the Met Office column is the Met Office's own blended site forecast (their open data). GREEN only when both agree it's a full offshore day (9+ hrs of 8am–8pm); amber when they split. The hourly table shows either app's numbers (switch above it) from 5am to 9pm; only 8am–8pm counts. An agreed day still drops to ☔ wet when either forecasts 0.5mm+ of rain for 3+ of the flying hours (rain still comes via Open-Meteo). ECMWF rides along silently so we can later check whose call comes true at each beach. Gusts are the least certain part — read it as a ranking, not a promise. Updates every 4 hours.
